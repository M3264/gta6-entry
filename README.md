Play as Rook, a driver climbing the criminal ladder in the sun-soaked city of Solmara. Explore on foot or by car, take jobs, and outrun the law.

## Run it

Run `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080.

## Controls

WASD or arrows move and drive; E enters or exits a car; mouse aims and fires; 1 and 2 select weapons; R starts a rampage; Escape pauses; Enter starts or restarts.

## Missions

1. First Impression
2. Hot Delivery
3. Collection Day
4. The Sun Vault
5. Moving Target
6. Crown of Solmara
